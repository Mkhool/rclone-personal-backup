Rclone Personal Backup

Personal Google Drive backup utility

This is a personal, non-commercial configuration of Rclone, used to back up and manage files in Google Drive accounts that I own or have permission to access.

Google sign-in is used solely to authorize Rclone's access to those accounts. The application is not offered as a public service.
