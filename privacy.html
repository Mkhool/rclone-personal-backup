Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Rclone Personal Backup is a personal, non-commercial configuration used exclusively to access and back up files in Google Drive accounts authorized by the operator.

Google user data

After Google OAuth authorization, Rclone may access, list, upload, download, organize, and delete files within the authorized Google Drive account, according to actions initiated by the operator. This configuration requests Google Drive access in order to manage backups and files.

Storage and security

Rclone runs locally on the operator's computer. OAuth credentials and tokens are held in the local Rclone configuration and are not published on this website. Backed-up files are stored in the authorized Google Drive accounts. Temporary files and cache may be stored locally during transfers.

Sharing and sale

This personal configuration is not a public service. The operator does not sell Google user data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising. File transfers use Google Drive and the locally running Rclone application.

Retention and revocation

Files are retained in the selected Google Drive account until the operator deletes them. Local authentication credentials may be removed from the Rclone configuration; access can also be revoked from the Google Account security settings. Revocation does not automatically delete files already uploaded.

Contact

Questions about this personal configuration may be directed to its operator through the associated Google OAuth consent screen contact details.

This page describes this specific personal Rclone configuration, not the privacy practices of Google, GitHub, or Rclone as independent services.
